# Code of conduct

re:Invent Compass is a community project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to make it a welcoming place.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to AWS, MCP or open source.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, and when someone represents the project in public, for example on AWS Builder Center or at a community event.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer, Hennie Francis, through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/henniefrancis/aws-re_invent-compass/security/advisories/new) or a direct message to [@henniefrancis](https://github.com/henniefrancis). Do not open a public issue. Every report is read and handled promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person who reports is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a participant, depending on the impact:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning that explains what was wrong.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** no further interaction with the project.
