# First-timer guide

These notes come from official AWS pages, checked on 17 October 2026. Each fact is followed by the page it came from. The full list of pages is in `references/official-sources.md`. If something here does not match what the official page says now, trust the page and tell the attendee.

Compass is an independent community project and is not affiliated with AWS.

## Session types

- Keynotes need no reserved seat. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Lecture-style breakout sessions are not reserved either. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Workshops are hands-on, in groups as small as 10 people. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Chalk talks are architects sketching solutions live, and code talks come with live demos. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/

## Reserved seats and walk-up

- Bootcamps, technical sessions, chalk talks, code talks and workshops can have reserved seats. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Favouriting a session does not secure a seat, and reserved seats cannot be passed to someone else. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- You cannot reserve two sessions that happen at the same time. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Be at the room at least ten minutes before the start. If your seat is released you join the back of the walk-up line, and a reserved seat is not guaranteed. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- The AWS Events app is where seats are reserved and where reserved and favourited sessions show together. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/mobile-app/

## Repeat sessions

- A repeated session has `-R` in its session id. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- If you cannot get a seat, check back for availability or join the onsite walk-up queue. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Compass uses this when it swaps a booking: it reserves the repeat first, then cancels the original.

## Badges

- Attendees must be at least 18 by the day they pick up their conference badge, and a badge and lanyard are needed in designated event spaces. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- The FAQ page does not say where or when badges are picked up. Do not guess. Send the attendee to the official page.

## Venues

- Session content is at Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, MGM Grand, The Venetian and Wynn. The dates section of the same page also lists Encore. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- The page advises leaving extra travel time between venues. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Conference venues, hotels and shuttles are collected on the official venues and hotels page, which is linked from https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/

## When you do not know

Say that you do not know and point to the official pages. Never invent a time, a place or a rule.
