The official rules of the re:Invent Catalog API Builder Challenge (version updated 24 September 2026, copied by the owner on 19 October 2026), each with how re:Invent Compass meets it. The TAM checks this table at the Go/No-Go (tracker G01) and again before submission (S01). If AWS updates the rules, re-copy them and re-check every row.

## Judging criteria and where Compass answers them

The four criteria come from the rules; the 25 percent weight each was recorded at kickoff from the contest page.

| Criterion                                       | Where it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Creativity and novelty of the integration       | The airline metaphor: repo to agenda, the week as a Kiro spec, hooks as guardrails, and Wallet boarding passes that show "Boarding closes" (corrected 26 October 2026: the pass no longer says "Seat released at"). |
| Utility for re:Invent attendees                 | First-timer focus: the 10-minute seat release, walking connections, clashes, meals and repeats, checked before every booking.                                                                                       |
| Technical depth and use of the API surface      | All 12 operations, PKCE sign-in on loopback, quota metering, a guardrail engine with 100 percent coverage, a serverless pass API on AWS.                                                                            |
| Quality of the published Builder Center article | D02: published on 5 Nov with the screenshots, the videos and a stats image, after the npm package was live; a second article gives the measured numbers.                                                            |

## Owner requirement: Kiro first (not a contest rule)

The rules do not require Kiro. The owner requires it anyway, to show the newest way of building on AWS (owner decision, 19 October 2026):

- The product is a Kiro Power: the Power, the week as a Kiro spec and the Kiro hooks are the primary experience. Other MCP clients are supported, but Kiro is the one the README, the site and the article lead with.
- The build follows Kiro spec-driven development. Every stream has a Kiro spec (requirements, design, tasks), and those specs are the single record of what was built, including the work Claude's agents did after the owner's Kiro credits ran out (ADR-029).
- Every change starts as a Kiro spec or a task in an existing spec, and Claude's agents build from it (ADR-029, confirmed by the owner on 19 October). Work done before its task was written is backfilled into the spec the same day.
- The owner uses Kiro for the demo, the Power and the article.
- The article and the demo video show Kiro: the Power in use, the spec of the week and a hook blocking a bad booking.
