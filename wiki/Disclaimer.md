## Passes are unofficial

- A Compass [boarding pass](Glossary#boarding-pass) is unofficial and is **not a ticket**. Your re:Invent badge is your ticket. Bring it to every session.
- Every pass says so on its back, carries no barcode, and cannot be shared from Wallet.
- A pass does not reserve or hold a seat. Only a reservation in your real re:Invent schedule does, and only until [boarding closes](Glossary#boarding-closes), 10 minutes before the start.
- Walking times are estimates, not official times. Check them yourself.

See [Board](User-Guide-Board#what-a-pass-shows) for everything on a pass.
